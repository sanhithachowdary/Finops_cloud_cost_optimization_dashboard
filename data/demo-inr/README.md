# Sample bills priced in Indian rupees

The same four generated bills as `data/demo/`, with every price and cost multiplied by the
configured rate (₹94.95 per $1) and `BillingCurrency = INR`. Usage, utilisation and the planted
scenarios are identical. Made with `python -m scripts.generate_sample_bills --currency INR`.
Upload any of them on the dashboard, or drag one onto `run_demo.cmd`; no conversion is needed.
